Transportation System Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Data protection practices for student commuter records and transit logs.

1. Information We Collect

The College Bus Seat Allocation System collects student registration identifiers, department affiliations, official institutional email addresses, assigned transit routes, travel intention declarations, and boarding point records. Access PINs are stored exclusively as irreversible cryptographic hashes (SHA-256).

2. Purpose of Processing

Personal commuter data is processed solely for vehicle fleet capacity planning, passenger seat allocation, route optimization, campus safety coordination, and passenger manifest auditing by authorized college transportation personnel.

3. Data Access & Role-Based Authorization

Access to student travel declarations and administrative controls is strictly restricted. Students may only access and update their own records using verified student identification and PIN authentication. Fleet administrators access centralized manifests through authenticated sessions.

4. Retention & Security

Daily travel declarations and passenger seat assignments are retained for the duration of the academic semester for logistical audit purposes. All database communications employ parameterized SQL queries to prevent unauthorized data manipulation.

5. Contact and Inquiries

For inquiries regarding transportation records or to request data corrections, contact the College Transportation Office via campus mail at transport-office@college.edu.
