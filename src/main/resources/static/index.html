Standard Operating Procedure for Students

  • All registered student commuters must submit a travel declaration for each scheduled college day.
  • Declarations can be updated without penalty up until 09:00 PM of the previous evening.
  • A unique access PIN prevents unauthorized modification of records.
  • Allocated seats are non-transferable and verified upon boarding.

Automated Capacity Allocation Rules

  • Greedy capacity algorithm matches declared passenger counts to available buses.
  • Strict vehicle dispatch rules prevent double-booking or overlapping assignments for any bus.
  • Insufficient capacity triggers immediate notifications to fleet dispatchers.
  • Each passenger is designated an individual seat number on their assigned bus manifest.
