Transportation Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Rules governing student travel declarations, seat allocation, and boarding regulations.

1. Declaration Cutoff Deadlines

Students must declare their daily travel intention before 09:00 PM of the evening preceding the scheduled travel date. For same-day declarations, changes close strictly 60 minutes prior to scheduled shift departure. Declarations submitted after the cutoff will not be accepted.

2. Seat Allocation & Boarding Policy

Seat assignments are generated by the algorithmic allocation engine based on vehicle seating capacity. Students must occupy only their assigned bus and seat number. Assigned seats are strictly non-transferable to other students.

3. Fleet Capacity & Contingency Scheduling

When commuter demand exceeds available bus fleet capacity, the system flags an insufficient-capacity alert. The transportation logistics department reserves the right to schedule additional trips, consolidate routes, or deploy auxiliary transport.

4. Passenger Code of Conduct

All commuters must present their digital seat confirmation or institutional identity card upon boarding. Inappropriate behavior or unauthorized boarding without an allocated seat may result in suspension of transit privileges.

5. System Modifications

The College Transportation Office reserves the right to adjust route stops, fleet assignments, or operational timings based on college academic schedules and campus requirements.
